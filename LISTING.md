# addons.mozilla.org listing text

**Name:** No More Rewatch for YouTube

**Summary (max 250 characters):**
Stops you from watching the same YouTube videos and Shorts again. A video you already watched is blocked with a full-page overlay. No "watch anyway" button.

**Description:**
No More Rewatch remembers every YouTube video and Short that you watch for 30 seconds (or half of a short video). When you open one of them again, the extension covers the page and stops the video.

Features:
- Blocks watch pages, Shorts and embedded players, on desktop and mobile YouTube.
- Block for a number of days, or forever. After you save the duration, you can only make it longer, so you cannot easily undo your own decision.
- Import the videos you already watched from your Firefox history or from a Google Takeout export (Chrome history or YouTube watch history).

Privacy: all data stays in your browser. The extension sends no data anywhere and has no analytics.

**Category:** Social & Communication (or: Privacy & Security / Other)
**License:** MIT
**Homepage / support:** https://github.com/tyjga29/no-more-yt-rewatch

**Notes to reviewer:**
No build step; the uploaded files are the source. The optional "history" permission is requested only when the user clicks "Import from Firefox history", and is used only to find YouTube video URLs in the history. Data is stored only in storage.local.
